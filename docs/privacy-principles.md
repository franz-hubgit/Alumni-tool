# Privacy Principles

Open Alumni Network is intended to connect students, alumni, and university communities while protecting personal information and maintaining trust.

## Privacy by Design

Privacy should be considered from the beginning of development rather than added later.

The platform should:

- Collect only the data needed for its core functions
- Use clear and understandable privacy settings
- Avoid making profiles publicly searchable
- Restrict access to verified community members
- Give users control over the information they share
- Protect sensitive personal and professional information
- Document how personal data is stored and used

## User Control

Users should be able to:

- Edit their personal information
- Choose which profile details are visible
- Control whether other members can contact them
- Leave a community
- Request deletion of their account
- Request a copy of their stored data

## Community Verification

Access to private communities should require verification.

Verification may be managed by:

- Society administrators
- Leadership-program administrators
- University representatives
- Trusted community moderators

## Data Minimization

The MVP should avoid collecting unnecessary data.

Examples of optional information include:

- Employer
- Job title
- Industry
- Location
- Areas of expertise
- Conference attendance
- Preferred forms of support

Users should not be required to provide information that is not essential to their participation.

## Funding and Support Requests

Funding and support requests should be transparent within the relevant community.

The platform should:

- Clearly identify the person or group making the request
- Explain the purpose of the request
- Record approval or moderation decisions
- Avoid exposing financial information publicly
- Provide a way to report suspicious or misleading requests

## Security

The platform should use appropriate safeguards, including:

- Secure authentication
- Role-based permissions
- Protected passwords and credentials
- Access logging where appropriate
- Regular dependency and security reviews
- Secure storage of personal information

## Future Work

Before a public production release, the project should complete a detailed privacy review and document its data-retention, deletion, export, and moderation procedures.
