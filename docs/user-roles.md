# User Roles

This document defines the initial user roles planned for the Open Alumni Network MVP.

## Student

Students are current members of a university society, leadership program, or student initiative.

Students can:

- Create and manage a personal profile
- Join a verified community
- Indicate which conferences or events they plan to attend
- Search for relevant alumni
- Request introductions or meetings
- View side events and networking opportunities
- Submit requests for funding or practical support
- Contact approved alumni within their community

## Alumni

Alumni are former members of a participating university society, leadership program, or student initiative.

Alumni can:

- Create and manage an alumni profile
- Add professional experience, location, interests, and areas of expertise
- Indicate which conferences or events they plan to attend
- Specify how they are willing to support students
- Respond to introduction or mentoring requests
- Offer funding, access, advice, venues, or professional connections
- Create or participate in alumni and student side events

## Society or Program Administrator

Administrators manage a specific university society, leadership program, or community.

Administrators can:

- Review and verify membership requests
- Manage community information
- Create or approve events
- Moderate profiles and support requests
- Remove inappropriate content
- Invite students and alumni
- Review reports from community members
- Manage community-specific permissions

## Platform Moderator

Platform moderators help maintain safety and trust across the wider platform.

Moderators can:

- Review reported content
- Investigate misuse
- Suspend or restrict accounts
- Support community administrators
- Enforce platform rules
- Review potentially fraudulent funding requests

## Platform Administrator

Platform administrators are responsible for the technical and operational management of the platform.

Administrators can:

- Manage platform-wide settings
- Create and remove communities
- Assign administrator and moderator roles
- Review security and system activity
- Manage integrations and platform configuration
- Support data protection and account-related requests

## Permission Principles

The MVP should follow these principles:

- Users should only access information required for their role
- Private profiles should not be publicly searchable
- Community membership should be verified
- Sensitive actions should require approval or moderation
- Funding requests should be transparent and traceable
- Users should control what profile information they share
- Administrative actions should be logged where appropriate

These roles may change after testing with students, alumni, and program administrators.
