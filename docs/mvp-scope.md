# MVP Scope

## Objective

The initial MVP will test whether a structured alumni platform can improve coordination between students and alumni attending major international conferences.

The first pilot use case focuses on a global leadership program whose students and alumni attend geopolitical, political, sustainability, and business conferences, including events surrounding the World Economic Forum and the UN Climate Change Conferences.

## Primary Users

### Students

Students participating in the leadership program who are attending or seeking to attend relevant conferences.

### Alumni

Former participants who may attend the same conferences or provide professional knowledge, introductions, mentoring, access, funding, or practical assistance.

### Program Administrators

Trusted organizers responsible for verifying membership, managing the community, and moderating activity.

## Core User Needs

Students need to:

* Find relevant alumni attending a conference
* Request an introduction or meeting
* Discover side events and networking opportunities
* Request practical or financial support
* Learn from previous attendees

Alumni need to:

* Indicate which events they will attend
* Specify how they are willing to help
* Discover relevant student initiatives
* Offer mentoring, introductions, or funding
* Participate in side events

Administrators need to:

* Verify community membership
* Moderate profiles and requests
* Manage conference and event listings
* Protect member privacy
* Review reports and inappropriate activity

## Included in the MVP

* Secure account registration
* Verified community membership
* Student and alumni profiles
* Conference listings
* Attendance indicators
* Search and filtering
* Introduction requests
* Support and funding requests
* Side-event listings
* Basic moderation

## Excluded from the Initial MVP

* Public social-media features
* Complex recommendation algorithms
* Direct payment processing
* Native mobile applications
* Public access to member profiles
* Integration with university databases
* Automated access to conference credentials

These features may be evaluated after the pilot.
