# Project Roadmap

Open Alumni Network is currently in its research and early prototyping phase. The roadmap below describes the intended development process and may change as the project receives feedback from students, alumni, and contributors.

## Phase 1: Project Definition

* Define the initial user groups and their needs
* Interview students, alumni, and university society representatives
* Map the core student and alumni user journeys
* Establish privacy, trust, and moderation principles
* Select the initial technology stack
* Develop low-fidelity interface wireframes

## Phase 2: Initial MVP

* User registration and authentication
* Student and alumni profiles
* Community and society profiles
* Conference attendance registration
* Alumni discovery by conference, location, and professional field
* Introduction and meeting requests
* Side-event coordination
* Funding and practical support requests
* Basic administrative and moderation tools

## Phase 3: Pilot Program

* Test the MVP with a global leadership community
* Support coordination around international conferences
* Collect structured feedback from students and alumni
* Review privacy, accessibility, and security
* Document findings and prioritize improvements

## Phase 4: Open-Source Expansion

* Improve contributor onboarding
* Create deployment documentation
* Add localization support
* Enable additional universities and societies
* Develop community governance processes
* Publish reusable templates and integrations
